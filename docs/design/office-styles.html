Which office style feels right?

Every agent is an NPC. What it does maps to live status: typing at a desk = working, a "!" bubble = waiting for you, in the break room = idle, red smoke = error, walking to another desk = delegating.

A. Top-down pixel office

One room per harness, desks per session. Like Pixel Agents and Stardew. Easiest to read at a glance, scales to many agents.

B. Isometric cozy office

Habbo / Two Point style, one floor split into zones. The prettiest option, but harder to build and agents can hide behind furniture.

C. Side-view office tower

One floor per harness, with a lift for delegations between harnesses. Fun "building" metaphor, but cramped once a floor has more than about 6 agents.

Either way, clicking an NPC opens its detail panel

Harness, provider, model, account, project folder, current tool step, tokens and value for the session, parent and child agents, and the limit bar of the account it uses.
